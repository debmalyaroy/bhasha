# Bengali course - points still open for the owner

The whole course (00-course.json and all 38 units) has been proofread end to end. No spelling,
grammar or verb-agreement error is known to remain. The points below are choices where two
defensible answers exist; each is consistent across the course as it stands.

## Romanisation choices (applied consistently everywhere)

1. **নও / হও written `nou` / `hou`** (11-negation phrase "তুমি একা নও", guide text of 11 and 15, key in
   00-course). The key tells the learner to say "no-o", "ho-o"; it clashes visually with `ou` = ঔ.
   The alternative is `no-o` / `ho-o`.
2. **ঙ্গ written `ng`, not `ngg`**: সঙ্গে `shonge`, একসঙ্গে `ekshonge`, মঙ্গলবার `mongolbaar`. The spoken
   form has a g (shong-ge); the conjunct note in 00-course says so. Kept because `shonge` is in a
   phrase carried over from the old course.
3. **অ্যা at the start of a loanword is a single `a`** (অ্যাম্বুলেন্স `ambulens`), while য-ফলা + আ after a
   consonant is `yaa` (ব্যাগ `byaag`, হ্যাঁ `hyaan`, ট্যাক্সি `tyaaksi`). `yaa` is also used for a real
   y + aa (দয়া `doyaa`, কুয়াশা `kuyaashaa`), so the roman line alone does not tell the two apart.
4. **এ said as the 'cat' vowel is still written `e`** (ek, keno, kemon, dekhaa, ekhon, eto, dey, nen);
   the difference is explained in `about` and in notes only.
5. **s versus sh in three words**: অফিস `ophish` (many say "ofis"); প্রশ্ন `prosno` ("proshno" is also
   heard); শ্রাবণ `sraabon`.
6. **বিজ্ঞান `biggaan`** (05-reading). Careful standard pronunciation has the 'cat' vowel here too
   ("biggyaan"); both are heard in Kolkata.
7. **পরশু `porshu`** (careful speech "poroshu"); **গ্রীষ্ম `greeshsho`** ("greeshmo" also heard);
   **স্মৃতি `sriti`** ("smriti" also heard). The last two occur in guide text only.

## Usage choices

- 07-pronouns: "আমাকে তুমি বলুন।" as the invitation to use তুমি. Correct and understood; the fuller
  "আমাকে তুমি করে বলুন" / "আমাকে তুমি করে বলবেন" is at least as common.
- 04-numbers: "একটা দিন।" (Give me one) can also be read as "one day" out of context.
- 37-dialogue-market: the prices (100 a kilo for mangoes) are only illustrative.

## Record: what changed from the old bn.json (verified by comparing the two)

- All ten original units keep their ids, and all 100 original phrases are still in their original
  units with identical `native`, `roman` and `english`, with one exception: family
  "আমার এক দাদা আছে।" was replaced by "আমার একজন দাদা আছেন।".
- `literal` was added to the original phrases, and a number of their notes were extended. Two notes
  were replaced or dropped: family "ইনি আমার স্ত্রী।" (the husband form is now its own phrase) and
  emergencies "আমাকে একটু সাহায্য করুন।" (বাঁচান! is now its own phrase).
- Letters: ং ঃ ঁ moved from Consonants to a new "Other signs" group (chandrabindu shown on আ as আঁ,
  hasanta on ক as ক্); conjunct and numeral groups added. No other letter entry changed.
- The course has 38 units rather than about 30: a reading unit (05), a classifiers unit (13) and
  three line-by-line dialogues (36-38) were added to the skeleton in SPEC.md.
