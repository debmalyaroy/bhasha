# Kannada course - points that remain open

The course was proofread line by line (00-course.json and all 34 units) and the romanisation of every phrase,
guide example and letter example was re-checked by script against the Kannada text (0 mismatches). The
spoken-form spellings and number forms that the writer had listed as uncertain were checked and are settled;
they are recorded at the end so nobody re-opens them. Only the points in the first two sections are open.

## Owner decisions (nothing is wrong; a choice was made)

1. **Romanisation of ಫ in loanwords: `f`, not `ph`** (all units): `fon`, `kaafi`, `foto`, `aafees`,
   `saaftver`. `ph` is kept only for the letter name and Sanskrit words (`phala`). Documented in `about`.
   If speech scoring relies on `ph`, revert these.
2. **Long e / long o are not marked** in the romanisation (ಎ/ಏ both `e`, ಒ/ಓ both `o`), as in the old course.
   So `kodu` = ಕೊಡು and ಕೋಡು, `ide` = ಇದೆ and ಇದೇ. The `about` section says so and notes flag the common
   long ones. Marking them would be more exact but changes the scheme the app already uses.
3. **Polite 2nd person ending -ೀರ, and wh-questions without the final -aa**: ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರ?, ಮಾಡ್ತೀರ, ಇದ್ದೀರ.
   This is correct spoken Kannada and keeps the rule "yes/no question = long -aa" clean, but ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಾ? and
   -ೀರಿ are at least as common in writing. Both are mentioned in notes. Change only if the owner prefers
   the more familiar-looking ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಾ?.
4. **Size**: 34 units and 499 phrases, above the "roughly 30 units / 400 phrases" target in SPEC.md.

## Minor doubts left alone

5. **Written 2nd person singular** is given as `iddeeya`, `maadutteeya`, `maaduttiddeeya` (05, 13, 14, guide
   text only). School grammars print ಇದ್ದೀಯೆ / ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀಯೆ (also ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀ); ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀಯ is widespread in print and is
   not wrong. Left as is.
6. **15-past, neuter 'it' forms in the spoken paradigm lines**: `kudiyitu`, `kottitu` are the full standard
   forms; in fast speech they contract further (ಕುಡೀತು, ಕೊಡ್ತು). The full forms are understood everywhere and
   are safer for a learner, so they were kept.
7. **16-future**: ಅವನು ನಾಳೆ ಹೇಳ್ತಾನೆ is translated "He will tell us tomorrow"; the Kannada has no "us". Natural
   in context, left alone.
8. **Loanword spellings** have accepted variants (ಟೊಮೇಟೊ / ಟೊಮೆಟೊ, ಇಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ / ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರ್, ಟೀವಿ / ಟಿವಿ, ಬಿಸ್ಕತ್ /
   ಬಿಸ್ಕೆಟ್). The ones used are all current.

## Settled in review (no action needed)

- Spoken past forms ಮಾಡಿದ್ವಿ, ಮಾಡಿದ್ರಿ, ಮಾಡಿದ್ರು, ಮಾಡಿದ್ಲು, ಬಂದ್ರು, ಬಂದ್ರಿ, ತಿಂದ್ರಿ, ಹೇಳಿದ್ರು, ಕೊಟ್ಲು, and in guide text
  hodlu, hodvi, kotvi, kotri, kotru, idlu, idvi, idri, idru, aadlu, aadvi: these are the usual spellings.
  The doubled consonant is written single before the ending (ಕೊಟ್ರು, ಇದ್ರು, not ಕೊಟ್ಟ್ರು, ಇದ್ದ್ರು).
- Spoken neuter plural ಅವು ಮಾಡ್ತವೆ and continuous ಅವು ಮಾಡ್ತಿವೆ: correct.
- ನೀನು ಮಾಡಿದೆ / ನೀನು ಊಟ ಮಾಡಿದೆಯಾ?: correct and not stilted; the fast-speech ಮಾಡಿದ್ಯಾ? was added to the note.
- Neuter present ಬರುತ್ತೆ, ಆಗುತ್ತೆ, ಹೋಗುತ್ತೆ (with ಬರತ್ತೆ mentioned as a pronunciation): fine.

- Numbers: 11-19, the tens, ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತಮೂರು / ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತನಾಲ್ಕು (the school-table forms; ಇಪ್ಪತ್ಮೂರು is the spoken

  naanu / nanage hogabeku: all acceptable as written.
